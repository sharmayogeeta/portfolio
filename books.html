Books

Two books drawn from the Mahabharata and the Bhagavad Gita — one for thoughtful adult readers, one for little hearts.

Non-fiction · Philosophy

The Battlefield Within

Every Mahabharata Character Lives in You

2026For adult readersAvailable on Amazon

Every character in the Mahabharata — Arjuna's doubt, Duryodhana's wounded pride, Karna's divided loyalty, Draupadi's demand for justice — is a living force inside each of us. This book meets them one by one, shows how they surface at work, at home and in grief, and turns to the Bhagavad Gita for how to choose which of them leads.

It is dedicated to the author's father, whose lifelong teaching inspired it: that all the characters of the Mahabharata live within you, and it is always your choice which one you play.
