Performative Power and Visual Discourse: A Media Study of High-Profile Interviews and Gendered Narratives

Yogeeta Sharma & Dr. Vijay Kumar · Vol. 7, No. 1s, pp. 20–26 · Published 17 February 2026

This study treats high-profile media interviews as staged performances in which power and gender are built both visually and through language. Using visual discourse analysis and a qualitative reading of interview transcripts, it examines how framing, body language, questioning strategies and visual cues shape gendered power relations — finding that women interviewees are asked far more questions about marriage, motherhood and appearance, while men are asked about achievements and plans.

Stronger Together: Crafting Successful Industry–Academia Partnerships

Explores how collaborations between universities and industry can be mutually beneficial while addressing the challenges they face. Using a mixed-methods approach — empirical data, case studies and theoretical analysis — the paper sets out benchmarks for judging partnership effectiveness and highlights the importance of aligned goals, realistic timelines, ethics and fair resource sharing.
