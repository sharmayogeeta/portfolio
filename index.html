About me

A writer at heart, a researcher by training

I have spent more than five years writing and editing for the web — SEO-led articles across gaming, sport, technology, lifestyle, automotive and legal topics — alongside teaching English and academic writing.

My doctoral research looks at power and gender in interview discourse, and I've published six papers in the field. More recently I've turned to books inspired by the Mahabharata and the Bhagavad Gita, for adults and for children.
